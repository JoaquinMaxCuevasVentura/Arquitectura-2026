# Cost structure for architectural renders and walkthrough video in La Paz, Bolivia (2025-2026)

> Research date: 2026-10-01. Pipeline confirmed by the user: Revit -> D5 Render (stills + walkthrough) -> Photoshop post -> AI realism enhancement -> video edit.
> **Method caveat:** Direct page fetching (WebFetch) was blocked by the network proxy for almost every domain (bcb.gob.bo, chaos.com, infobae.com, lostiempos.com, boliviaimpuestos.com, lapatria.bo, ferrere.com). All figures below come from search-engine result snippets/summaries that point to the cited URLs. The session's web-search budget also ran out before every sub-question was closed. Treat each figure as "reported by the cited page per search snippet" and re-check vendor pages and the BCB rate on the day of quoting.
> **Conversion rate used throughout:** 1 USD = **Bs 12.02** (BCB official rate published 2026-09-30, see the exchange-rate section). Pre-reform figures use Bs 6.96. Where Bs values are shown in parentheses they are my arithmetic, not the source's.

## 1. Software licensing costs (2025-2026, USD) for a Revit -> D5 -> Photoshop -> AI pipeline and alternatives

### Takeaway
The confirmed pipeline is cheap. D5 Render Pro is **US$360/yr** (or US$38/mo). Photoshop runs **US$19.99-22.99/mo**, and DaVinci Resolve is free (Studio is a one-time US$295). Before AI tools, the core software stack costs about **US$600-700/yr per seat** (about Bs 7,200-8,400). Revit is by far the most expensive item (**US$3,005/yr**), but CBI presumably already owns it because the model comes from CBI. D5's free Community tier is **non-commercial**, so a paid quotation needs D5 Pro.

### Inferences
- Minimum commercial stack for the confirmed pipeline, per seat per year: D5 Pro US$360 + Photoshop (Photography plan US$19.99 x 12 = about US$240) + DaVinci Resolve free = **about US$600/yr (about Bs 7,210 at 12.02)**. Swapping Photography for the Photoshop single app gives about US$636/yr. CC Pro gives about US$840/yr but includes Premiere/After Effects and unlimited standard Firefly generations.
- Software cost per productive hour: US$600/yr over about 1,600 productive hours/yr (assumption, see Labor) = **about US$0.38/h (about Bs 4.5/h)**. Allocating per project by months of subscription used is also reasonable: D5 Pro monthly is US$38, so a 1-month project costs about Bs 457 for D5 plus about Bs 276 for the Photoshop single app.
- Revit is needed only if the visualizer edits the model. If CBI supplies the .rvt and only LiveSync/export is needed, the visualizer still needs a working Revit seat (or CBI exports the model). This should be stated as a quote assumption.
- If CBI or the visualizer has under US$1M revenue, Twinmotion and Unreal are free for commercial use, which makes them zero-license alternatives to D5 Pro.

### Gaps
- I could not open d5render.com/pricing directly to settle the Community-tier resolution/watermark conflict. Confirm on the vendor page before quoting.
- I found no standalone 2025-2026 price for Chaos Vantage. The "US$65.90/yr" Collection figure is suspect.
- Prices are US list prices. Whether Autodesk, Adobe or D5 apply LATAM/Bolivia regional pricing could not be checked.

## 2. AI render-enhancement tools (Krea, Magnific, Topaz, ComfyUI, Leonardo, Photoshop Generative Fill, D5 AI, video AI): prices, credits, commercial terms

### Takeaway
A single paid AI enhancer plan (Krea Basic US$9/mo, Magnific/Freepik Premium about US$14.50/mo, or Topaz Studio about US$45/mo annual) covers a still-image job of this size. Upscaling the walkthrough video is the bigger cost: **Topaz Video US$299/yr (Personal) or US$699/yr (Pro)**. I found no reliable, independent figures for "time saved per image". Vendor claims (D5) are marketing.

### Cited Findings
- **Krea AI**: Free tier gives 100 compute units (CU) per day with basic upscaling to 2K. Basic is US$9/mo for 5,000 CU, with a commercial license and upscaling to 4K. Pro is US$35/mo for 20,000 CU, all video models, Nodes, and upscaling to 8K. Max is 60,000 CU at US$105/mo with upscaling to 22K. Business starts at US$200/mo for 80,000 CU and up to 50 members — [Krea pricing](https://www.krea.ai/pricing); [comparedge](https://comparedge.com/tools/krea-ai/pricing); [Krea AI upscaler page](https://www.krea.ai/features/ai-upscaler)

## 3. Hardware: render workstation prices in Bolivia, D5 GPU requirements, depreciation and electricity (DELAPAZ)

D5 officially recommends an RTX 3060 12GB-class GPU and 32 GB RAM. For animation and large scenes it recommends an RTX 3090-class (24 GB) card with 128 GB RAM. In Bolivia an **RTX 5070 12GB costs about Bs 9,400-11,600** and an **RTX 4070 Super about Bs 9,050** (about US$750-970 at 12.02). Prices of high-end GPUs (5080/5090) spiked worldwide in 2026 because of AI demand. Bolivian electricity is cheap (about **Bs 0.8-1.3/kWh**), so power is a minor cost line.

### Inferences
- GlobalPetrolPrices converted at Bs 6.96, so its USD values correspond to about **Bs 1.32/kWh commercial** and **Bs 1.26/kWh residential** (my arithmetic). Tariffs are set in Bs and have been nearly frozen, so in USD they have fallen to about US$0.11/kWh at Bs 12.02.
- Power cost per render hour, assuming whole-system draw (not sourced): about 0.4 kWh/h for an RTX 5070 workstation and about 0.65 kWh/h for an RTX 4090 workstation. At Bs 1.3/kWh that is **about Bs 0.5-0.85 per machine-hour**, which is negligible against labor.
- Indicative workstation for D5 animation: RTX 5070 Ti/4070 Super-class GPU (Bs 9,000-12,000) + CPU, 64 GB RAM, SSD, PSU and case (unpriced in Bolivia; assume a similar amount again). Total **about Bs 18,000-25,000 (US$1,500-2,100)**. This is an estimate, not a quote.
- Depreciation: 4 years (48 months) of about 1,600 productive h/yr, at Bs 20,000, gives **about Bs 3.1 per productive hour** (Bs 20,000 / 6,400 h), or about Bs 417/month.
- GPU class drives D5 animation time. Use the D5 statement that a 4090 is about 2x a 3090 to scale render-time estimates.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable Bolivian retailer listing for full workstations, CPUs or RAM in Bs in 2026, and no Eloy Salmón-specific price survey (search budget exhausted, retailer pages not fetchable).
- I found no 2025-2026 DELAPAZ "categoría general" (commercial) Bs/kWh tariff sheet. Latest specific Bs tiers found are from 2019.
- I could not verify the Bolivian tax depreciation rate for computer equipment in this session. It is commonly cited as 25%/yr (4 years) under the IUE regulation, DS 24051 annex; verify with SIN.

## 4. Cloud render farms and GPU cloud prices for animation

### Takeaway
Traditional farms bill CPU by GHz-hour (about 1.2-2.4 US cents) and GPU by OctaneBench-hour (about 0.4-0.5 cents). A 30-second 1080p V-Ray/Corona walkthrough costs about US$40-150 on a farm. **D5 Render is a real-time engine that standard farms generally do not run.** D5 animation offloading uses rented cloud GPU workstations instead. For a D5 job, local rendering on one RTX-class GPU is usually enough, so a farm line is optional.

### Cited Findings
- RebusFarm: CPU 1.24 US cents per GHz-hour; GPU 0.46 cents per OctaneBench-hour (OBh) — [RebusFarm pricing](https://rebusfarm.net/buy/products); [superrendersfarm comparison 2026](https://superrendersfarm.com/article/cheapest-render-farm-2026)
- GarageFarm: about US$0.024/GHz-h and about US$0.004/OB-h at low priority — [superrendersfarm](https://superrendersfarm.com/article/cheapest-render-farm-2026)
- Fox Renderfarm: GPU billed per node-hour, from US$1.80 (Ordinary) to US$0.90 (Diamond) on 2-card nodes, with the GPU model not disclosed. CPU about US$0.0306-0.051 per core-hour — [superrendersfarm](https://superrendersfarm.com/article/cheapest-render-farm-2026); [Fox price guide (2025)](https://www.foxrenderfarm.com/share/how-much-does-render-farm-cost-per-hour/)
- A 30-second 1080p walkthrough (900 frames) costs about **US$40-150** on a cloud farm with V-Ray/Corona CPU, and about US$18-55 with GPU Redshift — [superrendersfarm cost per frame](https://superrendersfarm.com/article/render-farm-cost-per-frame-guide)
- Lumion: about 3-6 hours to export a 1-minute 4K 30 fps video on a cloud RTX 4090 — [radarrender](https://radarrender.com/best-cloud-rendering-for-lumion-walkthrough-animation-export-4k-video-on-cloud/)
- D5 on cloud GPU for animation and walkthroughs is offered by GPU-workstation rental services rather than frame-based farms — [VFXRendering](https://vfxrendering.com/d5-render-on-cloud-gpu-for-animation-and-walkthroughs/); [iRender D5 article](https://irendering.net/d5-render-needs-a-powerful-gpu-you-dont-have-heres-the-workaround/)

### Inferences
- With D5 on a local RTX 4070-5070-class GPU, a multi-minute 1080p or 4K walkthrough renders in hours, not days (see the production-time section). A farm or cloud line in the quote is a contingency (for example "4K delivery on cloud GPU: US$X") rather than a core cost.
- If the visualizer used V-Ray/Corona for the animation instead, budget about US$80-300 per minute of 1080p (scaling the US$40-150 per 30 s) plus upload time.

## 5. Production-time benchmarks (model prep, stills, animation; D5-specific render times)

### Takeaway
Labor, not machine time, dominates. Industry benchmarks: about **16 artist-hours for a standard daytime exterior still** and about 24 h for complex or sunset views, with a range of 8-80 h. In D5 the GPU time per frame is tiny: about **0.3-0.35 s per 1080p frame on an RTX 4090**, which is about 10 minutes of render per minute of 1080p/30fps video. Machine time for a D5 walkthrough is hours, while offline engines (V-Ray/Corona) need 240-720 machine-hours per minute.

### Cited Findings
- Still images (artist time): "a standard daytime view takes about 16 hours of work; a large or sunset view runs from about 24 hours". The same exterior "can require anywhere between 8 and 80 hours of work" depending on complexity. Delivery is typically 48-72 h to 3-7 business days per still — [ArchiCGI project time](https://archicgi.com/cgi-services/architectural-visualization-project-time/); [Renderahouse](https://www.renderahouse.com/architectural-rendering-services); [Vinnomedia](https://vinnomedia.com/how-long-architectural-visualization-complete/) (search summary pooled these sources)
- International freelancer rates are about US$50-150/h. This is a US/EU market reference, not Bolivian — same sources as above
- **D5 on RTX 4090**: a 5-second, 60 fps, 1080p sequence rendered in 1 min 45 s; another 5-second animation took 1 min 26 s; a 2K still rendered in 19 s — [D5 RTX 4090 review](https://www.d5render.com/posts/nvidia-rtx-4090-d5-render-review); [iRender D5 on RTX 4090](https://irendering.net/how-does-d5-render-perform-on-rtx4090/). The search summary stated "about 21 s per frame", which is an arithmetic error: 105 s / 300 frames = about 0.35 s/frame.
- Render time depends on length, frame rate, scene complexity, lighting, materials and quality settings. There is "no single render time" for 1080p, 2K or 4K animation — [VFXRendering](https://vfxrendering.com/d5-render-on-cloud-gpu-for-animation-and-walkthroughs/)
- Lumion: a 1080p still takes under 1 minute on a strong GPU; a 4K still takes 8-15 min on an RTX 4090. 1080p has a quarter of the pixels of 4K, and render time drops about 60-70% — [radarrender](https://radarrender.com/how-long-should-a-lumion-exterior-render-take-benchmarks-and-bottlenecks/)
- Twinmotion: Standard and Lumen modes run in real time, while Path Tracer exports can take "many seconds, or longer" per frame, turning 4K exports into hours — [radarrender](https://radarrender.com/twinmotion-video-export-is-too-slow-heres-the-real-bottleneck/); [iRender](https://irendering.net/twinmotion-4k-exports-take-forever-faster-rendering-paths/)
- Offline engines: at 10 min/frame a 30-second clip (750 frames) is about 125 h on one machine. At 30 min/frame, 1 minute at 24 fps (1,440 frames) is about 720 compute-hours — [radarrender walkthroughs](https://radarrender.com/why-arch-viz-walkthroughs-take-days-to-render-and-how-to-speed-them-up/); [superrendersfarm](https://superrendersfarm.com/article/render-farm-cost-per-frame-guide)

### Inferences
- **D5 machine time per minute of video:** 1,800 frames (30 fps) x 0.35 s = about 10.5 min at 1080p on a 4090. At 4K (about 4x pixels) that is about 40-45 min. On an RTX 4070/5070 (roughly half a 4090; assumption based on the 3090 vs 4090 ratio), budget **about 0.5-1.5 machine-hours per finished minute** including re-renders. Even at high-quality settings it stays under a few hours per minute.
- Labor estimate for the Tupiza bus terminal (large public building with exterior plaza, platforms, landscaping). These are my estimates built from the benchmarks above, not sourced figures; the report writer should present them as assumptions:
  - **Model preparation / scene set-up** (one-off): LiveSync/import, material reassignment, fixing Revit geometry, site context, terrain, vegetation, vehicles/buses, people, lighting and sky: **24-48 h**.
  - **Per still**, once the scene exists: camera, composition, lighting, test renders, Photoshop post and AI enhancement: **4-8 h** for a real-time D5 workflow. Benchmarks of 16-24 h for offline engines include scene building, so the scene set-up is amortised separately here.
  - **Per minute of walkthrough**: camera paths, animated people and buses, phase/sun animation, test exports, final render, editing, music and titles: **16-32 h of labor** plus about 1 h of machine time.
  - Revision rounds: add 15-25%.
- Cost-per-unit formulas: **Cost per image = (prep hours / N images + hours per image) x loaded hourly rate + (software + hardware + power per hour x hours) + AI credits per image.** **Cost per minute = (share of prep hours + hours per minute) x loaded hourly rate + machine hours x (depreciation + power) + editing/music licenses.**

- No RTX 4070/5070-specific D5 animation benchmark was found.
- Stock music and licensing costs for the video were not researched.

## 6. Labor rates in Bolivia (2025-2026), minimum wage and employer social charges

### Takeaway
The national minimum wage (SMN) is **Bs 3,300/month for 2026** (DS 5516, +20%, retroactive to 1 Jan 2026). In 2025 it was Bs 2,750, derived from the +20%. Bolivian salary data for architects and 3D modelers is thin and partly dated. Junior architects earn about Bs 2,300-6,600/month; 3D modelers earn about US$600-1,000/month (2023 data). Employer social charges are **about 17.21%** on gross pay, plus aguinaldo and indemnización provisions (about 8.33% each).

### Cited Findings
- SMN 2026 is Bs 3,300 for the public and private sectors: a 20% increase, retroactive to 1 January 2026 and mandatory. It was set by DS 5516 (Gaceta Oficial 13 Jan 2026) and RM 088/26. Part-time work is prorated — [Unitel](https://unitel.bo/noticias/economia/decreto-el-salario-minimo-sube-a-bs-3300-su-aplicacion-es-obligatoria-y-rige-desde-el-2-de-enero-de-2026-CM18639182); [Gaceta Oficial DS 5516 PDF](https://s1.boliviaimpuestos.com/img/2026/01/24154524/Decreto_Supremo_5516.pdf); [La Razón, 2026-01-11](https://larazon.bo/economia-y-empresa/2026/01/11/tras-incremento-de-20-como-es-el-salario-minimo-de-bolivia-en-la-region/)
- Junior architect (Paylab): 80% earn between Bs 2,277 and Bs 6,557 gross per month. After 5 years of experience a junior architect earns about Bs 4,471. More experienced architects earn about Bs 3,000-8,000 or more — [Paylab Bolivia](https://www.paylab.com/bo/salarios/construccion-e-inmobiliaria/arquitecto-junior?lang=es); [consultelivia (2025)](https://consultelivia.com/sueldos/cuanto-gana-un-arquitecto-en-bolivia/)

The specific hourly values were not captured — [CDALP](https://cdalp.org.bo/2025/06/23/cdalp-presenta-proyecto-de-actualizacion-del-arancel-minimo-para-el-ejercicio-profesional-de-la-arquitectura/); [CDALP Reglamento del Arancel Mínimo PDF](https://cdalp.org.bo/docs/reglamento%20del%20arancel%20minimo.pdf)

  - To the Gestora/SIP: Prima de riesgo profesional 1.71%, Aporte patronal vivienda 2%, Aporte patronal solidario 3.5% (subtotal 7.21%).
  - To the health insurer (CNS/Caja de Salud): 10%.
  - Total **about 17.21%** of the worker's total earnings.
  - Sources: [misalariobo](https://misalariobo.com/aportes-patronales-bolivia/); [Planifica Consultores](https://www.planifica.com.bo/aportes-afp-caja-salud-bolivia-guia-empleado-empleador/); [Gestora employer guide](https://www.gestora.bo/archivos/bajar?id=3087)

Projections for July 2025-June 2026 were negative or near zero, so it is unlikely for 2026 — [finiquitojusto](https://finiquitojusto.com/noticias-y-reformas-laborales/el-doble-aguinaldo-en-bolivia/)

### Inferences
- Loaded monthly cost of an employee: gross x (1 + 17.21% contributions + 8.33% aguinaldo + 8.33% indemnización provision) is about **gross x 1.34**, before vacation and other benefits. At the SMN that is about Bs 4,418/month. For a mid-level visualizer at Bs 6,000 gross it is about **Bs 8,030/month**.
- Hourly rates from monthly pay. Bolivian law uses a 240 h/month divisor (30 days x 8 h) for the hourly wage. For costing, about 160-176 productive h/month is more realistic.

  - Bs 9,000 gross (senior visualizer): about Bs 69-75/h loaded (about US$5.7-6.2/h at 12.02).

A La Paz freelance visualizer plausibly charges about Bs 60-150/h (about US$5-12/h). This is an estimate from the salary data and loading above; I found no published Bolivian freelance rate.

## 7. Bolivian taxes on invoicing these services (IVA, IT, IUE, retentions; 2026 reforms)

### Takeaway
As of 1 Oct 2026, an invoiced service (con factura) still bears **IVA 13% "por dentro"** (included in the price; 14.94% effective on the net) **plus IT 3%** on gross income. Combined, that is **16% of the invoiced price**, so to net N before income tax you invoice N / 0.84 = **N x 1.1905**. Ley 1733 (27 May 2026) will move IVA to "por fuera" (13% on top of the net price), but only from the month after its regulatory Supreme Decree is published, and I could not confirm whether that is in force yet. The separate **ITF** (financial transactions tax) was **abolished** in 2026 (Ley 1717). IT 3% was not changed. Without a factura, the contracting company retains **15.5%** (IUE 12.5% + IT 3%).

### Cited Findings
- IVA nominal rate is 13%, applied "por dentro", with an effective rate of about 14.94% on the pre-tax base. IT is 3% on gross income, and companies pay both IVA and IT — [ivacalculator](https://ivacalculator.com/bolivia/iva-13-tasa-calculo/); [boliviaimpuestos IT](https://boliviaimpuestos.com/impuesto-las-transacciones-it/); [SIN IT leaflet](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/IT-18F.pdf)
- **Ley 1733 "de Transparencia y Alivio Tributario"** was promulgated 27 May 2026. Among other things it changes Art. 5 of Ley 843 so that IVA is 13% on the net price, shown separately on the invoice ("IVA por fuera"). Example: net Bs 240 -> IVA Bs 31.20 -> total Bs 271.20. That change takes effect **on the first day of the month following publication of its regulatory Supreme Decree**, and until then invoices stay "por dentro" — [boliviaimpuestos IVA por fuera](https://boliviaimpuestos.com/iva-por-fuera-ley-1733/); [SIN press note](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/nota_prensa/proyecto-de-ley-transparenta-el-iva-y-fija-tasa-efectiva-real-del-13/); [Pixilegal Ley 1733](https://www.pixilegal.com/normativa/ley-1733-2026-05-27)
- **Status conflict:** one search summary says a regulation "RND 102600000019" regulating Ley 1733 was published in June 2026, and inferred the IVA change "should have" applied from 1 July 2026. But the law requires a **Supreme Decree** (an RND is a SIN resolution), and other sources stress that nothing changes until that DS appears. The SIN was still adjusting invoice formats in September 2026 (RND 102600000034) — [boliviaimpuestos 100 preguntas](https://boliviaimpuestos.com/100-preguntas-sobre-la-ley-1733-de-alivio-tributario-todo-lo-que-necesitas-saber-antes-de-que-se-te-pase-el-plazo/); [SIN RND 102600000034 (Sep 2026)](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/RND-1026000000034.pdf); [SIN "inicia aplicación de la Ley 1733"](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/nota_prensa/el-sin-inicia-aplicacion-de-la-ley-1733-de-alivio-tributario-tributario/)
- The bill's proposed voluntary 5% bimonthly single tax (replacing IVA, IUE and IT for sellers with under Bs 400,000/yr) was reportedly **not included** in the promulgated Ley 1733 — [boliviaimpuestos 100 preguntas](https://boliviaimpuestos.com/100-preguntas-sobre-la-ley-1733-de-alivio-tributario-todo-lo-que-necesitas-saber-antes-de-que-se-te-pase-el-plazo/). This conflicts with an SIN press note announcing a "Nuevo Régimen Tributario Especial... pago único del 5% bimensual" for small suppliers, which may predate the final law — [SIN](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/nota_prensa/nuevo-regimen-tributario-especial-permitira-formalizar-a-pequenos-proveedores-con-pago-unico-del-5-bimensual/)
- The Ley 1733 tax-debt regularization deadline was extended to 31 Dec 2026 by DS 5708 — [boliviaimpuestos](https://boliviaimpuestos.com/ley-1733-ds-n-5708-una-decision-que-favorece-a-los-contribuyentes-el-gobierno-amplia-el-plazo-de-regularizacion-tributaria-hasta-el-31-de-diciembre-de-2026/)
- The ITF (Impuesto a las Transacciones Financieras, created 2006) was **eliminated by Ley 1717** (President Rodrigo Paz, 2026), and the SIN repealed its regulation (RND 102600000013). This is a different tax from the IT 3% — [SIN](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/nota_prensa/impuestos-ratifica-eliminacion-del-itf-en-beneficio-de-la-economia-de-los-bolivianos/); [eju.tv, Apr 2026](https://eju.tv/2026/04/que-era-el-itf-y-por-que-su-eliminacion-marca-un-cambio-en-bolivia/)
- The government also announced eliminating taxes on gambling, business promotions and large fortunes — [Red Uno, Nov 2025](https://www.reduno.com.bo/economia/cuales-son-los-cuatro-impuestos-que-seran-eliminados-por-el-gobierno--2025112522331)
- **Retention when a natural person provides a service without a factura:** the payer withholds IUE 12.5% + IT 3% = **15.5%** (forms 570 and 410) — [boliviaimpuestos retenciones por servicios](https://boliviaimpuestos.com/retenciones-por-servicios-ejemplo-practico/); [yo-facturo](https://yo-facturo.com/blog/retenciones-iue-it-bolivia/); [SIN form 570](https://siatinfo.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/declaraciones-juradas-en-formato-electronico/impuesto-a-las-utilidades-de-las-empresas/570-iue-retenciones). One search summary instead claimed the rate "changed to 16% (RC-IVA 13% + IT 3%)", citing an Urenda Abogados note on independent professionals — [Urenda](https://www.urenda.bo/blog/tributario/principales-modificaciones-al-tratamiento-tributario-para-personas-naturales-que-ejercen-la-profesion-u-oficio-en-forma-libre-o-independiente-y-personas-no-domiciliadas-en-el-estado-purinacional-de-bo). I could not verify this. The 16% (RC-IVA + IT) figure is the standard rate for *rentals* (alquileres), and the summary may have conflated the two.
- DS 5563 sets 2026 tax incentives; details were not captured — [boliviaimpuestos DS 5563](https://boliviaimpuestos.com/ds-5563-incentivos-tributarios-2026-bolivia-resumen/)

### Inferences
- **Quote structure for CBI ("precio con factura", current IVA por dentro):** Precio facturado = (Costo de producción + Utilidad) / (1 - 0.13 - 0.03) = **(C + U) x 1.1905**. IUE (25% on net profit, standard Ley 843 rate, not re-verified here) is paid later on annual profit and is usually absorbed in the margin. CBI can take the 13% IVA as fiscal credit, so a "con factura" price is not 16% more expensive for CBI in real terms.
- **If "IVA por fuera" is in force at invoicing:** Precio neto = (C + U) / (1 - 0.03) (assuming IT stays at 3% of the net income, which is unverified). The invoice total is Precio neto x 1.13. The resulting total is about the same as the current formula (1.1649 vs 1.1905, depending on the IT base). Check the SIN before invoicing.
- **"Sin factura" (freelancer paid by CBI):** CBI withholds 15.5%. If the freelancer wants a net amount N, the gross is N / 0.845 = **N x 1.1834**. Many Bolivian quotes state two prices ("precio con factura" / "precio sin factura"), with the gap roughly 16-19%.
- Foreign SaaS subscriptions (D5, Adobe, Krea, Topaz) paid by card do not generate Bolivian IVA fiscal credit. Treat them as cost at their Bs card-charge value.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether the DS regulating "IVA por fuera" had been published, or its effective month, as of 1 Oct 2026. The SIN site was not fetchable.
- IUE 25% and the alícuota for profesionales independientes (Régimen General vs RC-IVA) were not re-verified in this session.
- I did not verify whether Bolivia taxes foreign digital services (an IVA on platforms) in 2026.

  - By August 2026 commentators described a shift from a flexible to a managed/administered float — [Los Tiempos column, 2026-08-06](https://www.lostiempos.com/actualidad/opinion/20260806/columna/bolivia-tiempo-record-paso-tipo-cambio-flexible-tipo-cambio)
  - In mid-2026 President Paz said the dollar had traded at about Bs 18.50 a year earlier and was "below Bs 10.50" at the time — [Red Uno](https://www.reduno.com.bo/noticias/que-dijo-rodrigo-paz-sobre-el-dolar-estas-fueron-sus-declaraciones-202671512756)
- **Cards and USD payments:** from April 2026, ASFI re-enabled online purchases and payments abroad with debit and credit cards after about 3 years of restrictions. Credit cards are limited only by the client's capacity; debit cards have about a **US$500/month** limit, expandable by each bank. USD charges are converted at the BCB referential rate "without additional surcharges". The measure covers 2.7M+ users and 8M+ cards — [ASFI](https://www.asfi.gob.bo/node/1362); [El Deber](https://eldeber.com.bo/economia/liberan-pagos-tarjetas-compras-internet-exterior_1775490132); [Red Uno](https://www.reduno.com.bo/economia/vuelven-los-pagos-online-desde-hoy-puedes-comprar-y-pagar-en-el-exterior-con-tarjeta-20264762230)

- Before April 2026, apps and wallets (P2P/USDT) gained ground for foreign purchases — [El Deber](https://eldeber.com.bo/economia/compras-exterior-aplicaciones-ganan-terreno-tarjetas-bancarias_1772660846)

### Inferences
- For quoting, convert USD software at the **BCB TCO on the quote date (about Bs 12.0 now)**, not 6.96. Add a validity clause (for example "precios válidos 15 días; montos en USD convertidos al TCO del BCB del día de facturación"), given the about 23% move from 9.73 to 12.02 in three months.
- Since the official and parallel rates had converged by end-September 2026, there is little remaining "dólar paralelo premium" to build into software costs. Bank fees for foreign card use were not researched.
- A D5 Pro annual license (US$360) cost about Bs 2,505 at 6.96 and about Bs 4,327 at 12.02, a 73% increase in Bs terms. Software lines in Bs-denominated quotes have risen sharply even though inflation is moderate.

## 9. Inflation in Bolivia 2025-2026 (INE) and its effect on service prices

### Takeaway
2025 inflation was **20.40%** (INE), the highest of the 21st century. In 2026 it has slowed sharply: **3.01% accumulated Jan-Aug 2026** and **5.02% year-on-year to August**, after a 4.82% first half that included a 2.13% monthly spike in May (protests/blockades) and a July decline. For a quote valid a few weeks, inflation is a minor factor next to the exchange rate. Bs-denominated labor costs have already absorbed the +20% SMN increase.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 full-year inflation was 20.40%, the highest since 1988 (21.47%) and above 2008 (11.85%). 2024 was 9.97%. ### Inferences
- The July drop (accumulated from 4.82% to 1.89%) implies a July monthly CPI change of about -2.8%. This coincides with the first month of the new FX regime, plausibly because the import-tariff and customs measures and post-blockade normalisation lowered prices. That is a hypothesis, not a sourced finding.
- Pre-2026 salary benchmarks (Paylab, the 2023 modeler data) should be scaled up by roughly 20% (2025) + 5% (2026 YoY), which is about +26%, to approximate 2026 Bs levels.
- Suggested quote practice: hold Bs prices for about 15-30 days. Index USD-linked items (software, cloud, hardware) to the BCB TCO, and keep Bs labor fixed.
